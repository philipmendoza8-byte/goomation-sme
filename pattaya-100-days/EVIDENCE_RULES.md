# Evidence Rules — Pattaya 100 Days

This file controls how the Pattaya 100 Days draft is written.

## 1. Evidence first

A statement must be traceable to one of the following:

- dated field record
- photograph / video
- operational document
- booking / service proof
- route or location record
- OTA product or platform working record
- verified working document
- human-confirmed note

If support is missing, write **UNKNOWN**.

## 2. Keep evidence streams separate

Do not merge these into one source:

- KOOLCustom / S_003 fabrication evidence
- Reflex801 foundation / system-origin context
- Golden Dragon / KOOL Car Rent service operations
- OTA product / content alignment

Cross-reference is allowed. Substitution is not.

## 3. No retrospective invention

Do not write later RePlat language as though it was already fully defined during every earlier event.

Later interpretation may be stated only as later interpretation.

## 4. AI is not the source of truth

AI may structure, compare, summarize and draft.

AI output is not evidence by itself.

## 5. Human review before main

All material remains on the draft branch until Philip reviews it.

## 6. Privacy / company boundary

Do not publish:

- private company documents
- credentials
- private customer information
- private booking data
- internal company assets without permission
- unverified claims of ownership or authorship

## 7. Narrative rule

Do not turn field evidence into motivational writing.

Write as:

- field record
- chronology
- evidence map
- operational note
- case reconstruction

Keep the rough edges when the evidence has rough edges.
